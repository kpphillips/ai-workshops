Privacy

What this site knows about you

Very little. These workshops are a free personal education project by Kevin Phillips. The only thing measured is how many people visit and how far through a workshop they get, with no way to tell who you are.

  • No cookies. This site sets none, and the analytics script uses none.
  • No personal data. No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no profiles.
  • Your API key stays with you. It lives in your browser's memory and goes only to OpenRouter.
  • No ads, no selling. There are no advertisers, and no data to sell.

What is counted

The site uses Umami Cloud, a privacy-focused analytics service. Umami says it does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information. It reports aggregate page views, referrers, browser and device type, and the country a visit came from.

On top of page views, the workshops send a few small events so Kevin can see which parts are useful. Each event carries only fixed labels such as a workshop name, a module number, or a lab name. Nothing you type is ever included.

Connect events record that a press happened. They never include the key, its length, or any error text.

What is never sent to analytics

  • API keys, or anything derived from them.
  • Prompts, lab inputs, pasted text, or model outputs.
  • Anything else you type into a page.

The site does not use fingerprinting, and it does not load any other analytics or tracking scripts. If you block the Umami script with a browser extension, every page and lab still works the same.

Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies. Some pages use your browser's local storage to remember your palette, your light or dark choice, and which checklist items you ticked. These values stay on your device, are never sent anywhere, and carry no identifier. Clear your site data to remove them.

Your OpenRouter key

The live labs call AI models through OpenRouter using a key you paste in. The key is held in your browser's memory for that tab only. When you reload, close the tab, or press Disconnect, it is gone.

Your browser sends the key, along with the lab's prompt, directly to OpenRouter. It does not pass through a server run by Kevin, and it is never sent to analytics. OpenRouter handles those requests under its own terms and privacy policy, and live runs spend real money on your account, so set a credit limit on any key you use here.

Some workshops have an Advanced section where you can change the endpoint. If you type in a different address, your key goes there instead, so only do that with a service you trust.

Other services involved

  • Umami Cloud receives page views and the events above.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Google sees your IP address when your browser fetches them, as with any font host.
  • The page host serves these files and may keep ordinary server logs under its own policy.
  • OpenRouter is contacted only when you connect a key and run a live lab.

Ads and money

The site is free, has no ads, and is not monetized. No data is sold or shared for marketing.
